# StrideOS Identity And Brand

## What StrideOS Is

StrideOS combines:

- endurance science
- strength programming
- recovery analytics
- movement quality
- coaching intelligence

Primary user:

The hybrid athlete.

Someone who:

- runs
- lifts
- wants longevity
- wants performance
- does not want burnout

This application is built from the perspective of:

- Doctor of Physical Therapy
- Strength and Conditioning Coach
- Running Coach

## Training Philosophy

Aerobic base first.

80/20 polarized training.

Hard days hard.

Easy days easy.

Avoid junk volume.

Progressive overload.

Deload intelligently.

Strength builds durability.

Metrics are only valuable if they change behavior.

The app should not say:

Your VO2 max increased.

without answering:

- What does this mean?
- What should I do next?

Every insight follows:

Observation -> Interpretation -> Recommendation

## Brand Feel

StrideOS should feel premium, calm, athletic, and intelligent.

It should be clinical in reasoning but not medical in tone.

It should feel like the athlete is being coached, not judged.

Avoid shame, punishment, streak-chasing, and generic motivation.

Prefer adjustment, explanation, and confident guidance.
