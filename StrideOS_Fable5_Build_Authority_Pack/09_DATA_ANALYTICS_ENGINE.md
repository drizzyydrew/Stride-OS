# Data Analytics Engine

Analytics must drive decisions.

No meaningless graphs.

Every chart or metric should answer:

- What happened?
- Why does it matter?
- What should I do?

## Required Areas

- Mileage trends
- Strength consistency
- Training load
- Readiness
- Recovery
- Pace trends
- Workout completion
- Movement quality trends

## Insight Pattern

Use:

Observation -> Interpretation -> Recommendation

Example:

Observation:

Mileage increased 20% over the last two weeks.

Interpretation:

Load is rising faster than the usual safe progression window.

Recommendation:

Keep the next run easy or reduce volume if readiness is low.

## Data Confidence

When data is sparse, say so.

Do not overstate insights from limited history.

Use confidence language:

- high confidence
- moderate confidence
- low confidence

Avoid fake precision.
