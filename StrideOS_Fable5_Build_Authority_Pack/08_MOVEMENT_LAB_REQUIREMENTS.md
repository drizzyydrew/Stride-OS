# Movement Lab Requirements

Movement Lab should preserve clinician quality.

It should feel like a physical therapist translated movement findings into training decisions.

## Required Fields

Track:

- Cadence
- Foot strike
- Overstride
- Crossover gait
- Hip drop
- Knee valgus
- Notes

## Required Output Pattern

Every finding should produce:

- Finding
- Meaning
- Recommendation

Example:

Finding:

Cadence is low for current pace.

Meaning:

Lower cadence can increase braking forces and overstride risk for some runners.

Recommendation:

Use short cadence strides after easy runs and cue quick, quiet steps during form work.

## UX Standard

The checklist should be fast to complete.

The output should be useful to a runner.

Avoid jargon without interpretation.

Do not over-medicalize normal movement variation.

Do not claim injury causation from one observation.
