Use `assets/movement-lab/dion/dion_reference_side.png` as the primary visual
reference and follow `DION_CANONICAL_REFERENCE.md`, `camera_rules.md`, and
`movement_lab_design_system.md`.

Both images must show the same canonical Dion, stationary road bicycle or
indoor trainer, room, lighting, camera, crop, and subject scale. The view is
direct lateral with the tested leg closest to camera. Keep Dion's full body,
both wheels, trainer, crank, pedals, hands, feet, saddle contact, and handlebar
contact visible.

The bottom phase should place the tested crank near 6 o'clock without locking
the knee. The top phase should place it near 12 o'clock with plausible hip and
knee flexion. Dion wears the canonical black shirt, black shorts, and white
shoes, with the tested shoe visibly contacting the pedal.

Reject:

- Three-quarter or 45-degree camera views.
- Cropped head, wheels, hands, feet, crank, or pedals.
- Warped bicycle geometry, floating shoes, or implausible pedal contact.
- Identity, anatomy, wardrobe, room, or lighting drift between phases.
- Extra people, outside logos, text, joint markers, or permanent angle overlays.

Both images passed the Build 38 full-resolution review for canonical Dion
identity, direct-lateral framing, full bicycle visibility, visible rider-bike
contact points, and distinct top/bottom pedal phases. The source PNGs do not
