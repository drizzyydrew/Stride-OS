# Movement Lab Branding Rules

These rules are permanent for all generated Movement Lab assessment assets unless a later approved version of this design system explicitly changes them.

## Character identity

- Use canonical Dion for every demonstration image.
- Read [`../dion/DION_CANONICAL_REFERENCE.md`](../dion/DION_CANONICAL_REFERENCE.md) in full.
- Supply at least one of the canonical Dion PNGs as an actual image reference for every generation.
- Preserve Dion's face, apparent age, Black identity, medium-dark skin tone, hairline, tapered fade, beard, athletic build, proportions, and default wardrobe.
- Do not assign or visually imply a canonical height, weight, or body-fat percentage.
- Do not replace Dion with another model, a lookalike, a stock photograph, or a generic silhouette.

## Visual identity

Movement Lab assets use the Moore Movement visual identity and StrideOS design language:

- Calm, precise, credible, athletic, and approachable.
- Training-focused rather than medicalized, futuristic, or theatrical.
- Natural human texture and realistic anatomy.
- Clean composition built for movement inspection.
- Restrained neutral palette: black clothing and equipment, white shoes, warm gray environment, natural green plant, natural skin tone.
- No dramatic gradients, neon light, colored rim light, sci-fi effects, or decorative visual noise.

## Canonical wardrobe

- Plain fitted black short-sleeve athletic shirt.
- Plain black athletic shorts above the knee.
- White athletic running/training shoes.
- Neutral ankle socks.
- No visible external branding.
- No jewelry, watch, hat, headphones, tattoos, or accessories.

Only an assessment-specific request may approve different footwear or clothing. Any approved change must remain plain, fitted, logo-free, and compatible with joint visibility.

## Canonical environment

- Neutral modern training room.
- Matte dark rubber flooring.
- Warm gray or greige walls.
- Minimal black dumbbell rack.
- One simple green plant.
- Clean, uncluttered background.
- No other gym users.
- No mirrors that create duplicate limbs or confusing reflections.
- No external brand marks, signage, posters, or readable text.

Equipment required by the assessment may be added. It must be plain, neutral-colored, realistic, and identical across all phases of the asset set.

## Lighting and color

- Use soft, even, natural-looking lighting.
- Keep exposure, white balance, shadow softness, and light direction consistent across the complete set.
- Skin tone must remain natural and consistent across the face, trunk, arms, hands, legs, and feet.
- Do not grey out hands, feet, limbs, or the far side of the body.
- Do not introduce random red, blue, purple, or green skin casts.
- Black clothing must retain enough detail to reveal trunk, hip, and shoulder contours without becoming crushed silhouettes.
- White shoes must retain shape and sole detail without clipping to featureless white.

## Anatomy quality

- Dion must have exactly two arms and two legs.
- Hands, fingers, feet, joints, and shoes must be anatomically coherent.
- No extra limbs, third limbs, missing limbs, fused anatomy, duplicated fingers, or mirrored body parts.
- No warped shoes, broken floor contact, floating feet, or impossible weight distribution.
- Exercise posture must be biomechanically plausible for the named phase.
- Never hide anatomy defects with cropping, equipment, darkness, text, or overlays.

## Source-image cleanliness

- Do not bake text, labels, arrows, joint markers, angle arcs, measurement lines, scores, grids, or coaching callouts into source images.
- Do not add StrideOS or Moore Movement logos inside assessment images unless a future brand specification explicitly requests a separate branded derivative.
- Keep canonical and assessment source images clean so the app can render accessible, dynamic overlays independently.
- Do not place watermarks or signatures in generated assets.

## Language and claims

When an image brief includes accompanying copy:

- Use `estimated`, `may affect`, `worth monitoring`, `consider addressing`, and `manual review recommended` where appropriate.
- Do not use `bad form`, `causes injury`, `high injury risk`, `diagnosis`, or guaranteed outcome language.
- Do not imply that a single 2D image is a clinical diagnosis or exact three-dimensional measurement.

## Branding rejection criteria

Reject an asset for any of the following:

- Dion identity drift or a different person.
- Changed skin tone, face, hair, beard, age, or major body composition.
- Random wardrobe changes, external logos, tattoos, jewelry, or accessories.
- Different room layout, flooring, wall color, plant, rack, or lighting within a set.
- Skin discoloration, grey extremities, or inconsistent color temperature.
- Any anatomy defect, extra limb, fused joint, or warped shoe.
- Text, watermark, permanent overlay, or unapproved logo.
- A visual tone that is theatrical, medicalized, cluttered, or inconsistent with Moore Movement and StrideOS.
