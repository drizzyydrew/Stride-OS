Frequently asked

How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen, choose Forgot password?, enter your account email, and tap Send Reset Link. Check your inbox and spam folder, then open the newest StrideOS reset email. Allow it to open StrideOS, choose your new password, and sign in. An expired or already-used link needs a fresh request. If the app stays on Finishing sign in, keep it installed, check that you have the latest available build, and contact support with the build number and displayed message. Never send your password or reset link.

Do I have to train for a race?

A new goal is optional. When a training block ends, use Goal archive to review its recorded training summary and add a race result if you have one. A block ending does not mean you completed a race. Choose a new goal when you are ready, or choose Keep training without a plan to continue with open training. Your saved workout history remains available.

How are my training zones calculated?

Pace targets can use a race result or estimated profile data. Heart-rate zones depend on the selected method and available inputs; a supported field test can help update calibration. Check the source and method labels in your profile. These are training estimates, not a medical assessment or a guarantee that a target is appropriate for you.

What is the daily check-in for?

The check-in records how you feel and supports the app's readiness guidance. Treat the result as a training estimate. A check-in does not itself give permission to share information with an AI provider or guarantee an automatic plan change.

Do I need to use AI Coach?

AI Coach is optional. Before using it, review the sharing explanation and choose whether to allow messages and selected training context to be sent to Anthropic. AI responses can be wrong, and access is subject to usage limits and service availability. On-device Insights do not require that AI-sharing permission. See the privacy policy for details.

Can I switch between miles and kilometers?

Open Settings and choose Imperial or Metric under Units. Check each activity's unit labels when comparing recorded and estimated values.

The GPS stopped recording during my run.

Check StrideOS location and background permissions in iOS Settings. Missing permission, interrupted recording, signal loss, and device state can affect tracking. Do not delete the saved activity to force a repair; contact support with the error and build number.

I reinstalled the app and my data is gone.

Sign in with the same account and check whether its training backup restores. Failed or incomplete backups cannot recreate missing records. Local movement clips and analyses are not automatically cloud-backed-up; contact support before resetting or reimporting records.

How do I delete my account?

In app versions with Account & Legal, open Settings, then Account & Legal, then Request account deletion and follow the available confirmation flow. Automatic processing is currently disabled while release acceptance continues. If your build or account cannot submit a request, email mooredrew5@gmail.com to arrange identity verification and deletion assistance. Keep the visible request reference if one is shown; never email the private recovery secret. Reset Plan and uninstalling are not account deletion.

My deletion request is pending, or the app is locked.

In versions with secure recovery, a saved confirmation, a recorded server request, server completion, and local cleanup are separate stages. Normal training and sync stay locked during processing. Keep StrideOS installed, connect online, unlock the device, and use Retry status and cleanup. If the screen says server deletion is complete but local cleanup is not verified, retry cleanup; do not assume it already finished. Follow the app's close-and-reopen instruction after verified cleanup. If you need help, send the visible reference, build, and displayed stage without private credentials.

Why is Apple or Google identity confirmation requested?

A pending provider disconnection can need fresh credentials from the same account. Use the app's explicit Confirm Apple identity or Confirm Google identity action when offered. If a processing attempt is still active, retry status later; repeated identity prompts do not repair server configuration. Cancelling an optional identity prompt does not cancel a previously confirmed deletion choice. When old credentials are unavailable, the app may show manual provider-connection instructions instead of claiming automatic revocation.

What remains after deletion?

A minimal server completion record and a protected device recovery record remain for status and cleanup verification, as described in the privacy policy. Apple Health records, photo-library originals, copies sent to Strava or other services, and local copies on other devices are not erased by this device's cleanup. Deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage renewal in Apple subscriptions. No fixed processing deadline or immediate backup purge is promised.
