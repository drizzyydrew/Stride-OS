Common Questions

How do I reset my training data?

Go to Profile → Data & Privacy → Reset All Data. This permanently removes all training history from your account.

I reinstalled the app and lost my data. Can I recover it?

If you were signed in before reinstalling, your data is backed up to your account. Sign in with the same email and your history will restore automatically.

How do I cancel or delete my account?

Email us at mooredrew5@gmail.com and we'll delete your account and all associated data within 48 hours.

The GPS tracking stopped during my run. What happened?

Make sure "Always" location permission is enabled in your iPhone Settings → StrideOS → Location. This allows GPS to continue when the screen is locked.

My video won't upload in Movement Lab.

Videos require an active internet connection and a signed-in account. Check your connection and try again. If the problem persists, email us with your device model and iOS version.
