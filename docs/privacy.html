Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

StrideOS is a personal training application for runners. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Data We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address via Supabase Auth, a third-party authentication service.

Training and health data. Workout logs, strength sessions, check-ins, heart rate data, pace zones, race PRs, and other fitness information you enter in the app. This data is stored on your device (AsyncStorage) and, when you are signed in, backed up to our Supabase database.

Movement videos. Videos you upload to Movement Lab are uploaded to Supabase Storage and stored in a private bucket accessible only to your account. Videos are not stored on your device after upload.

Location data. If you use GPS Run Tracking, the app requests access to your device's location. Location data is used only to calculate distance and pace during your run and is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit raw GPS coordinates to our servers.

AI Coach messages. Messages you send to the AI Coach are transmitted to Anthropic's Claude API via a secure Edge Function proxy to generate responses. We do not store the content of your AI Coach conversations on our servers.

Optional usage and diagnostics. Both are off by default and can be controlled independently in Settings under Privacy & Diagnostics. If you opt in, PostHog receives a small allowlist of basic app and tutorial events with a randomly generated, session-only identifier. It receives no account identifier, health or workout measurements, routes, messages, photos, or videos. Sentry receives sanitized JavaScript exception types and code locations, not original error messages, account identifiers, screenshots, attachments, or native crash dumps. Neither service records your screen or automatically captures its contents.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To power the core features of the app — training plans, analytics, zone calculations, and progress tracking.
  • To back up your training history so you don't lose data if you reinstall the app.
  • To provide Movement Lab storage and professional analysis services when requested.
  • To authenticate your account and keep your data secure.
  • With your separate opt-in choices, to understand basic feature use and diagnose software errors. Service connections necessarily expose a network IP address to the receiving infrastructure; disabling location enrichment does not make these connections anonymous.

We do not use your data for advertising. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

StrideOS uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — Authentication, database, and file storage. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic (Claude) — AI responses in the Coach tab. Messages are processed by Claude. Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • Apple HealthKit (optional) — With the permissions you grant, StrideOS reads health and workout data for training features and restoration, and writes completed workouts to Apple Health. HealthKit content is not sent to PostHog or Sentry.
  • Strava (optional) — If you connect Strava, completed workouts are uploaded to your Strava account. Strava Privacy Policy
  • PostHog (optional) — Basic product usage events after you enable usage sharing. PostHog Privacy Policy
  • Sentry (optional) — Sanitized JavaScript diagnostics after you enable error reporting. Sentry Privacy Policy

4. Data Retention

Your training data remains in our database as long as your account is active. You can delete all data at any time from Profile → Data & Privacy → Reset All Data. This permanently removes your account data from our servers.

Turning optional sharing off stops new reports and discards the app's unsent session data. It does not recall requests already transmitted or delete previously received vendor reports. No analytics or diagnostic offline queue is persisted by StrideOS. Contact us for requests about previously submitted reports; the reporting identifiers do not contain your account identity.

5. Children's Privacy

StrideOS is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Your Rights

You may request a copy of your data, correction of inaccurate data, or deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the address below.

7. Security

All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Data at rest is stored in Supabase with row-level security policies that ensure you can only access your own data. Movement videos are stored in a private bucket accessible only by authenticated requests from your account.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
