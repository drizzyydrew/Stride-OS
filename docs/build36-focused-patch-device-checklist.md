# Build 36 Focused Patch — Device Checklist

Use a physical iPhone on the uncommitted Build 36 patch before assigning the next iOS build number.

## Movement Lab capture

- [ ] Open video capture and confirm the front camera is selected by default.
- [ ] Flip to the rear camera during setup; confirm the preview changes and the choice remains through capture.
- [ ] Confirm live preview appears before any countdown begins.
- [ ] Confirm the required direct front, side, or back view, visible-joint list, full-body framing, Dion guidance, and movement duration are readable without obscuring the athlete.
- [ ] Press Start Recording or Take Photo and confirm the five-second countdown begins only then.
- [ ] Confirm camera flip is unavailable during countdown and recording.
- [ ] Confirm video stops automatically at the movement-specific duration.
- [ ] Confirm Review supports Retake and Use Video/Photo; cancel at each stage and verify partial media is discarded.
- [ ] With the front camera, confirm the saved review is identified as unmirrored and anatomical left/right matches the athlete.

## Movement Lab analysis

- [ ] Analyze a direct side squat/run and confirm only the closest-side hip-knee-ankle chain renders.
- [ ] Change closest side and confirm overlay, angle chart, key frames, saved result, and AI Coach handoff all change together.
- [ ] Analyze a direct front single-leg-control clip and confirm supported bilateral frontal markers appear without sagittal knee/hip-flexion claims.
- [ ] Scrub and play a video; confirm marker overlay, chart cursor, and selected key frames remain synchronized.

## Running and hydration

- [ ] Select each fueling interval (15, 20, 30, 40 minutes), restart the app, and confirm the selection and Race Mode summary persist.
- [ ] Run a shortened debug cadence for each interval if available; pause/resume around a cue boundary and confirm only one cue fires.
- [ ] Create and select a named custom run for today, tap Not Today, restart the app, and confirm the scheduled run is restored while the custom run remains available.
- [ ] Restore the custom run, edit it, and confirm its name and parameters reload without adding a completed workout log.
- [ ] Enter distance, duration, temperature, and humidity manually; leave and return to confirm persistence and no weather snap-back.
- [ ] Turn current-location weather back on and confirm temperature/humidity refresh from the device location.
- [ ] Confirm the plan shows fluid, carbohydrate, sodium, reminder interval, duration, weather, execution notes, and limitations with correct mg/L versus mg/hr labels.
