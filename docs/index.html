Your training, connected.

Bring your sessions and training history together, whether you’re building a running routine, focusing on strength, or balancing both.

Run

Make room for your next run.

Plan running sessions and record supported outdoor activities with GPS. Review your saved distance, pace, and splits. GPS conditions and connected devices can affect the measurements available.

Lift

Strength belongs in the week.

Keep strength alongside your running. Log weights and completed sets, then look back at saved sessions to see the work you’ve done.

Recover

Check in with yourself.

Check in on your sleep duration and quality, how your body feels, your energy, and your stress levels. Use your check-in and training history to reflect on today’s session. Readiness is an estimate, not a medical assessment.

A goal when you want one.

Choose a race or a running, strength, or hybrid focus. When a block ends, choose what comes next—or continue in open training, recording workouts at your own pace without a generated plan. A new goal is optional.

A record of the work behind you.

Completed or replaced training blocks have a goal archive with a saved summary. Add a race result if you choose, revisit the block, and keep your activity history as you move on. An archived goal does not assume you finished a race.

More context. Your choice.

Review movement or ask a training question, with clear limits on what the tools can tell you.

Movement Lab

Review saved clips and images with supported movement-analysis tools. Camera-derived angles are approximate two-dimensional estimates, not a medical assessment.

Local clips and analyses are not automatically backed up to the cloud. See the privacy policy for how media is handled.

Optional AI Coach

Only after you explicitly opt in, messages and selected training context are sent to Anthropic’s Claude through StrideOS for fitness guidance. You can turn sharing off in AI Coach Privacy.

AI responses can be wrong. Usage limits and service availability apply. You can use tracking and on-device Insights without AI sharing.

StrideOS provides fitness information, not diagnosis, treatment, or a guarantee of injury prevention. For medical concerns, speak with a qualified clinician.
