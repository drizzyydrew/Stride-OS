# Build 38 Physical-Device TestFlight Checklist

## Inherited stabilization

- Front camera default; repeated front/rear switching; video/photo readiness.
- Five-second countdown only after Start; automatic stop; retake/use/cancel.
- Front-media mirror metadata and anatomical left/right correctness.
- Older and iCloud video import; scrubber ownership; locked marker edit.
- Closest-side overlay/chart/key-frame/Coach filtering.
- Hip flexion/extension naming and deep-squat values.
- Route name keyboard visibility; route detach/reattach and free-run mode.
- Hydration/fuel planning, persistence, separate voice intervals, combined cue,
  pause/resume deduplication, live location and manual weather.
- Training Block and Preset details, resume, finish, history, Live Activity.
- Morning readiness schedule, custom days, denial recovery.

## Cross-training intake

- No cross-training; one preference; multiple preferences; seasonal preference.
- Edit preferences and verify future programming changes without history loss.

## Calendar and Activity

- Mixed-activity calendar icons/names; Today replacement/supplement explanation.
- All filters; manual activity; applicable metrics only.
- Whole-body and dimension loads; no running pace/PR/mileage contamination.

## Walking and beginner plans

- Track and manually log a walk; route attach; walking directions.
- Walking-primary programming; run/walk active state and spoken transitions.
- Choose all four goals; minimum target date; accelerated acknowledgment.
- Remove a goal, preserve history, then select a new goal.
- Pace coaching toggle, smoothing, cooldown, and non-shaming wording.

## Outdoor activity and directions

- Cycling/hiking/skiing GPS, active time, distance, speed/pace, HR, elevation.
- Pause/resume, background, screen lock, incoming call, save, discard.
- Walking and cycling MapKit steps, voice turns, off-route state.
- Manual route breadcrumb wording and route preservation after provider failure.

## Live Activity

- One-tap pause, resume, and complete; visible pending state; rapid-tap suppression.
- Running, walking, run/walk, cycling, hiking/skiing, and strength content.
- Strength exercise, sets, reps, and load readability.
- Lock Screen, compact/minimal/expanded Dynamic Island, narrow devices, StandBy.
- App/ActivityKit synchronization after backgrounding and app relaunch.

## Bike Fit

- Dion card and both phase images; direct-lateral capture guidance.
- Camera capture and video import; top and bottom pedal-frame selection.
- Closest-side analysis, marker editing, charts, saved analysis, AI Coach handoff.
- Confirm conservative limitations and absence of complete-fit/diagnostic claims.

## AI Coach

- Primary goal, mode, cross-training, recent activity/load, upcoming work, and
  Bike Fit context remain question-relevant and below 5,000 characters.
