# Working name and fallback direction

Owner direction, November 3, 2026: keep StrideOS, while exploring more
distinctive alternatives if needed. This is a working-name decision, not a
trademark clearance or authorization to spend, file, contact another developer,
register handles, publish promotion or rename the app.

## Does Run, Lift, Recover make the name different?

It distinguishes the full listing text. The saved Apple draft visibly uses
**StrideOS: Run, Lift, Recover**. Another live Health & Fitness listing uses
**StrideOS**, with the subtitle **Sprint Training Platform**, from Bredenkamp &
Kury Development Group LLC: [Apple listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/strideos/id6755837942).
That listing also describes workout logs, AI insights and coach tools, so the
observed overlap extends beyond spelling into related product functions.

Apple asks developers to choose a unique app name in
[App Review Guideline 2.3.7](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#accurate-metadata).
A saved draft title does not establish eventual review acceptance.

Trademark similarity considers the overall commercial impression and related
goods/services. Descriptive additions do not necessarily resolve similarity
where the dominant wording is shared. See the
[USPTO explanation](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion)
and [current TMEP 1207.01(b)](https://tmep.uspto.gov/RDMS/TMEP/print?href=TMEP-1200d1e5395.html&version=current).

Assessment: Run, Lift, Recover explains the training scope but leaves StrideOS
as the apparent principal brand. Treat it as differentiation, not clearance.
Capitalization, punctuation, a different seller and a different app ID also do
not by themselves settle the rights question. No infringement, priority or
ownership conclusion has been reached, and no name change is yet proven necessary.

## Fallback exploration

These are creative working ideas only. None has been searched for existing
use, trademark conflicts, App Store availability, domain or social availability.
They are not cleared names or purchase recommendations.

| Working idea | Intended fit | Question to test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Formward | Forward progress across running, strength and recovery | Does it sound broad enough for athlete and coach workflows? |
| Pacewoven | A connected training routine and lasting history | Does pace make it sound too running-specific? |
| Moventra | A broad movement platform | Is the spelling easy to hear and remember? |
| Trainvero | Training clarity and consistency | Does it feel credible without implying clinical validation? |

Keep the current identifiers, domain, source, keys and assets intact. If a
fallback becomes necessary, screen preferred candidates before any rebrand or
spend. A federal search is only part of a clearance search;
[USPTO guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching)
also calls for broader sources. The planned international footprint would need
its own appropriate review. No professional engagement is commissioned here.

Technical launch preparation can proceed under the current working name.
Public-name clearance remains unresolved before new promotion or brand spending.
