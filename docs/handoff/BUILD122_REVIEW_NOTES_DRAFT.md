## Proposed reviewer notes

StrideOS combines running, strength and recovery tools. Sign in with the demo account supplied in the App Review Information fields. Do not use the developer's personal training account.

StrideOS Pro is one auto-renewable membership, offered monthly or annually. It unlocks creation of new personalized training goals, automatic week rebuilding and personalized AI Coach chat. Existing saved plans, history, workout recording, calendar, manual changes and on-device Insights remain available without Pro. Membership is at More → Settings → Account & Legal → StrideOS Pro membership. The price, billing period and any eligible trial are shown before Apple's confirmation. Restore purchases is on that page; closing the app does not cancel membership. Manage/cancel uses Apple's subscription settings.

AI Coach requires separate consent before messages and the selected training context are sent to Anthropic. Pro allows up to twenty requests per UTC day, subject to availability. Ask Coach has a compact requests-used-today disclosure with remaining allowance, reset time and an example. Revoking AI consent stops new AI requests. On-device Insights do not require AI consent.

To inspect training, create a separate new goal and choose running/lifting experience, equipment and available days. Get faster introduces faster work conservatively based on available training evidence. Plyometric options depend on experience and reported tolerance. A Norwegian 4×4 session is not prescribed for every user. Existing goals retain their saved programming; started and completed work is preserved. Adjust exercise can alter remaining work or provide a suitable alternative without rewriting performed sets.

An outdoor workout uses location permission. The optional paired Apple Watch app records supported metrics after starting from iPhone; unavailable sensor samples are shown as unavailable. No outdoor hard workout is required for review. During an active or paused session, Live returns to that same workout. Story sharing can show either a route illustration or an Everyday energy equivalent in the same region, never both together. Energy equivalents are estimates, not dietary instructions or direct measurements.

Apple Health, camera, photos and connected services are optional. Denying optional permission does not require enabling AI sharing. StrideOS does not diagnose conditions, guarantee injury prevention or promise clinical outcomes. Privacy and terms are linked from membership/settings.
