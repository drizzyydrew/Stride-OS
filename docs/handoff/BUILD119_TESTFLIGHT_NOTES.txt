Build 119 — Live Activity logo

The Lock Screen Live Activity now uses the approved three-chevron StrideOS logo with a transparent background, sized to fit beside the activity title.

After updating without uninstalling:
1. Start a brief activity and lock your iPhone.
2. Confirm the three chevrons appear beside the title with no gray or brown square.
3. Check the dimmed Lock Screen too, if your phone supports it.
4. Pause, resume and stop normally.

No outdoor run, new subscription purchase or repeat of the full Build 118 checklist is needed for this logo check. Do not reset your training data.

This update changes the logo assets and rendering only. Watch connectivity, recording, metric freshness, saved history, sign-in and membership policies are unchanged. The fictional marketing screenshot examples are kept outside the app and were not written into your account.

Public App Review and release remain separate from this internal TestFlight update.
