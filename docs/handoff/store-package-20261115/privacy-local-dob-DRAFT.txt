Proposed clarification for the existing privacy notice; not published:

An optional date of birth entered through the profile is stored on the device to
calculate age. StrideOS excludes that structured date from its cloud-profile
payload and automatic coaching context. Device or operating-system backups may
contain the local date. Information you deliberately type into an AI message is
handled under the separate AI-sharing terms and consent. Removing the profile
date keeps the current age used for training and does not erase historical
workouts or independently managed operating-system backups.

Reconcile the existing general backup wording against the actual data paths
before publishing this clarification. No new off-device DOB collection is implied.
