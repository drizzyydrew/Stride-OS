## Prelaunch social post

Your training week can include a run, a lift, and a day to recover.

I'm building StrideOS to bring those sessions into one routine: plan your week,
record workouts, and look back at your training history. When a goal ends, keep
its training summary and choose what comes next. Training without a race plan
is an option too.

StrideOS is currently in private iOS beta. Public signup is closed while launch
checks continue.

See what we're building: https://drizzyydrew.github.io/Stride-OS/
