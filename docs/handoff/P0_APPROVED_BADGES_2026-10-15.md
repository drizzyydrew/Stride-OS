## Archival 5K Correction

The old `sips` rasterizer ignored opacity for text: fill 0.76 could become alpha
255 rather than 194, and stroke 0.72 could become 255 rather than 184. The retained
5K lettering is now outlined using CoreText-verified AvenirNext-Heavy. Source
paint attributes and non-lettering geometry are unchanged, but historical PNG
pixel parity is **not** claimed. This badge was removed from the active Challenges
presentation at the user's request; its identity/history are retained.

Across 168 lettering comparisons, maximum ink-bound drift was one pixel. Reported
archival differences: maximum alpha 32.539%, ink mass 28.281%, mean PNG channel
difference 1.1947%. The new SVG and PNG agree exactly. These documented archival
differences do not apply to the active approved 600 exports. Validation reports
the variance rather than asserting that the historical raster is unchanged.
