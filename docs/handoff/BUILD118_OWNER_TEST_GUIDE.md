# Build 118 owner checks

Update through the existing internal TestFlight group without uninstalling, resetting data, or changing your normal StrideOS account. This is a membership/plan-access change, not a Watch/GPS/Health rewrite. Accepted recording/offline tests need not be repeated solely for this scope.

1. Open More → Settings → Account & Legal → StrideOS Pro membership. Plans should load automatically. Your current same-account Pro should be checked automatically; if active, the page shows active status and a Choose your training goal button. Do not press Subscribe again. Restore purchases is for recovery, not a step each time you reopen.
2. Close/reopen normally, revisit membership and confirm it checks access again automatically. Verify your existing plan, calendar, archived block and saved activities are still accessible. Pending/unavailable verification should offer a retry rather than claiming a new subscription.
3. If you want to change your goal, open Choose your training goal, review the form and only save if that is the goal you intend to activate. Existing setup choices may prefill after a new onboarding flow. Opening membership or purchasing never silently replaces a goal. Back out if you are just inspecting.
4. If a new purchase is genuinely needed, use Apple's explicitly marked no-charge TestFlight sheet. Check that the local price, period and eligible trial agree with that confirmation. The 14-day configuration was saved in Apple for the existing five markets; metadata can take up to an hour to propagate. An account that already redeemed a trial may be ineligible. Do not reset your main account to force eligibility.

## Separate release acceptance

An inactive/expired account needs its own deliberate acceptance scenario; do not delete or repurpose your main training account. New personalized goal creation/automatic week rebuilding should be gated, while existing plan/history/recording, safety/manual adaptations and existing-week repeat remain usable. Cancelled auto-renewal should retain access until actual entitlement expiry.

The deployed AI billing policy is still globally off pending authenticated RevenueCat/server guard acceptance. A client Pro badge does not prove that test. Source tests, local layout checks and TestFlight availability do not establish public-launch readiness. Signup/reviewer/deletion, production paid lifecycle, actual AI cost/capacity, genuine Store screenshots, final public binary/metadata and public release remain separate gates.
