# Build124 focused owner checks

These checks inspect ordinary app behavior. No hard workout, fabricated training entry or repeated subscription purchase is needed. Delivery and device acceptance are separate; this guide is not proof that124 is already available.

1. Open Strength → Preset Library. Recommended retains a focused list; All remains available. Muscle/equipment/time filters still apply. A selected heavier strength preset shows its main-lift reps, target effort and full rest; the detail and started prescription should agree.
2. Check a preset appropriate to your lifting experience and current schedule. If the heavier form does not fit, Review session fit explains why and offers a lighter option. Existing active workouts should continue their captured prescription.
3. Inspect a separate person-first strength goal without overwriting your current plan. Its block focus explains accumulation versus intensification. Heavy work is earned from actual recent comparable history, so a new goal/account may correctly remain in calibration rather than immediately show heavy weights.
4. At a rep-range or exercise/setup change, the old incompatible working weight should be unset for calibration. Do not invent completed work to unlock progression. Completed/previous workouts should retain their original sets, loads and prescriptions.
5. Review a normal recovery or shorter session. It should remain manageable, fit the time budget and retain readable instructions. Strength support should not replace your running-specific goal work.

If a check differs, report the screen, goal, current phase and what happened. Do not share passwords or perform a maximum lift for testing.
