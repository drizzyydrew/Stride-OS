# Build125 programming, calculators and Movement Lab access

The owner requested an explanation before implementation, then authorized commit, push and TestFlight delivery. No public App Review submission or release is part of this change. No exercise artwork or source spreadsheet was changed.

## Strength dose and specificity

The previous 2 × 3–5 means two working sets of three to five reps. RPE8 estimates about two good reps remaining, not 80%1RM. It can therefore represent a heavy load. Two sets were a conservative entry/calibration choice, but applying that dose permanently to practiced-lifter strength presets was too broad.

Designated strength preset main lifts now default to three working sets, with an explicit standalone choice of three, four or five. Only the designated mains change; accessories retain their purpose. The displayed prescription, started session and full-rest duration share one resolver. Captured active workouts retain their original dose. The automatic goal plan still starts new heavy calibration with two sets and earns its third from actual comparable work; a successful captured third set now survives the review week. Recovery and time constraints can still reduce the dose. A calendar date does not earn load or volume.

The [2026 ACSM position stand](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12965823/) supports heavier loading when maximizing strength, progressive stimulus and individualization, with diminishing additional strength benefits beyond roughly two to three sets. The older [NSCA Foundations of Fitness Programming tables](https://www.nsca.com/contentassets/693872c8a25245a7ba49fc5527a236b9/foundations-of-fitness-programming-quiz-preview.pdf) give useful goal-specific examples, not a universal requirement for five sets. Common rep ranges overlap. A 70%1RM load can improve strength; the app does not require a universal 80–85% average across every accessory, rehearsal, recovery week and main lift. Power also depends on movement speed and recovery, not simply maximum load. Push Press now precedes the more fatiguing work in the total-body power preset, and power presets receive experience/recovery/time guidance.

## Rep-max calculators

Settings → Strength calculators provides four advisory tools: estimated1RM, target rep-max weight, estimated repetitions, and percentage loads. Every tool has a separate worked example. Calculation details show the chosen method, named equations, disagreement range and assumptions. Estimates never update workouts, history, readiness or calibration automatically.

The owner's [One Rep Max Calc spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1N2_rhg1GgKkQZkYTfsGuvGtoOreFQzYf1D3b5kYDrhY/edit), tab One Rep Max Calculator (gid655951260), was read with bounded metadata/formula/value requests and left unchanged. Seven forward equations and their separate inverse weights are averaged explicitly. Expected repetitions use the three populated formulas; the fourth referenced cell is blank and is not invented. Wathen corrects the Sheet's spelling only. Original arithmetic, including differences at one repetition, is retained. Optional reported RIR is labeled as an added estimate, not an original Sheet feature or planned-RPE inference. Unit/load-convention changes clear weights rather than silently relabeling them. Formulas assume exercise/setup-specific external loads; unsupported bodyweight/timed tasks are not assigned misleading percentage targets.

Fixtures include 100 × 5 →115.4900949 seven-equation average versus116.6666667 Epley; a200 maximum and five target reps →173.2491831 average target-RM weight. These are explanatory inputs, not user performance records. High-repetition estimates, equation ranges and repetition-limit loads carry plain-language limitations. The education card distinguishes effort from percentage and summarizes overlapping strength, hypertrophy, endurance and power ranges.

## Plyometrics

The existing easy/medium/hard, optional task framework remains, with quality, actual landing response, next-day feedback, running demands, recovery and full rests governing progression. The latest relevant unfinished or skipped attempt can no longer disappear before eligibility is evaluated, exposing older favorable work. Adverse feedback from actual contacts remains visible even when the parent workout is unfinished/skipped, and those actual contacts count toward the current weekly ceiling. No completed work, symptoms or contacts are fabricated.

Sources reviewed include [NSCA plyometric guidance](https://www.nsca.com/education/articles/kinetic-select/plyometric-exercises/), the ACSM stand above, [Doc Lyss hybrid training](https://doclyssfitness.com/hybrid-training-made-simple/), [The Movement System Plyometrics101 public page](https://www.themovementsystem.com/plyometrics-101-info), and [KILO annual/undulating periodization](https://trainkilo.com/blogs/inside-the-system/undulating-periodization-annual-training-plan). The prior full KILO public-article audit is recorded in KILO_PROGRAMMING_BLEND_20261121.md. Paid courses and credentialed certification were not claimed. Exact app tiers, questionnaire thresholds, 42-day history window and individual-foot contact accounting are StrideOS coaching/product choices, not an endorsed NSCA/ACSM protocol or medical clearance.

## Session fit, layout and paid access

Session-fit guidance offers a lighter alternative, Cancel and Continue anyway for one session. Acknowledgement binds the exact displayed dose/context and the owner lease; changed sets/readiness/account require fresh review. Hydration, active-workout identity and cross-domain conflicts remain protected. This does not disable future guidance or bypass authentication/paid access.

Expanded AI usage paragraphs now live in a width-constrained Yoga sibling below the native disclosure header, avoiding RNHostView intrinsic paragraph width. The server allowance and counting/reset behavior are unchanged. Calculator details use the same bounded layout.

New Movement Lab capture/import, readiness assessments, analysis and first gait generation require verified Pro. Saved clips/results, playback, corrections and deletion remain available. Creation deep links are gated, async work is tied to the selecting owner/screen, and processing/save/upload boundaries recheck membership. Same-owner refresh is awaited; owner switches invalidate/remount work. This is an app access boundary, not a new server-side entitlement guarantee for owner-only storage. No backend, subscription price, trial or quota change is claimed.
