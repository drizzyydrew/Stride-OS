## Working Understanding

StrideOS is a connected adaptive training system for running, strength, recovery,
movement quality, and coaching. It should answer what to do today, why, and how
it helps, while respecting athlete agency, sustainable progression, actual data,
specific approved design, and working native behavior. Hybrid positioning does
not exclude beginners, walkers, or people adapting training.

The council is meant to carry Drew's documented decisions forward, not pretend
to know his mind, flatter him, or turn past assistant suggestions into doctrine.
It combines product/first-principles, athlete/outsider, training/evidence,
architecture/data/privacy, platform/release, design, business/expansion,
contrarian, and execution/chair lenses, selected for each decision. Independent
review and cross-examination precede consequential verdicts.

The existing design council and badge-builder remain the detailed visual and
production specialists. This general council does not replace them.

## Cross-Examination Outcome

Three read-only reviewers examined product intent, technical/release history,
and external-source/authority boundaries, then challenged the combined thesis.
The Chair adopted four revisions:

1. Approved daily flames can celebrate consistency without pressuring training
   through needed recovery; preserve accepted art and review incentives separately.
2. Current user choices outrank stale preference documents; implementation and
   assistant summaries are not automatic proof of founder intent.
3. Local-first/user-scoped code does not establish deployed privacy; a watch
   confidence score does not establish physical-device reliability.
4. Proportionate evidence should enable authorized work, not create a permanent
   deferral process. Future coach/athlete video direction remains historical,
   not implemented capability or authorization for a platform rewrite.

Potential policy/copy drift was found in coachEngine, App Store copy, and a
previously documented Live Activity emitter pattern. The evidence reference
lists exact paths. These were not reproduced or fixed in this documentation task.

Historical device verification remains Build90 evidence. HEAD/config92 is a
local checkpoint, not a claim about current EAS, Apple processing, or devices.
No commits, pushes, builds, submissions, or releases are authorized by this file.
