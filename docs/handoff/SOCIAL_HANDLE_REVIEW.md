## Material naming overlap

Apple's direct [StrideOS listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/strideos/id6755837942)
identifies a Health & Fitness app with the subtitle Sprint Training Platform,
seller Bredenkamp & Kury Development Group LLC. Its linked
[developer website](https://www.strideos.us/) describes training for track athletes.
This differs from Drew's App Store Connect app ID 6782788130.

Those primary pages establish another product using the same name in a related
category. They do not establish trademark registration, priority, infringement,
Apple's eventual review decision or the user's right to use a handle. No
trademark clearance search or legal opinion has been completed.

## Council decision

Keep existing source, domain, keys, receipts and draft store configuration.
Continue technical/support preparation. Hold public launch copy, new public
handle adoption and additional brand spending for a naming review with the
owner; consider qualified trademark counsel before committing to a final name.
No rebrand, new domain, filing, paid search or contact with the other developer
is authorized by this review. The preparation work remains useful whichever
public name is ultimately accepted.
