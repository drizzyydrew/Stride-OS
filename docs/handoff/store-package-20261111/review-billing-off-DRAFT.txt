Sign-in is required. Use the dedicated review credentials supplied privately in App Review Information. If initial fitness-profile setup appears, use fictional information.

Apple Health, location, notifications, AI sharing, usage sharing and diagnostic reporting have separate controls. Optional permissions can be declined; manual logging and saved-history review remain available.

Navigation: Today shows weather, current streak and training. Training options expands secondary goal and archive actions. Calendar shows scheduled/completed sessions. Running > Active starts supported outdoor recording, including open training without a plan. More opens Activity, Strength and Settings. Profile name/username editing opens from the profile header; the photo control is separate. In More > Settings, Goal archive opens Training archive. Users can choose another goal or continue open training. At a section root, Home returns to the top of Today.

For manual logging, open More > Activity, use Log activity, choose Walking, enter a fictional duration, leave optional measurements blank and choose Save. The saved detail should open. Test entries are manual inputs, not measured GPS or heart-rate data.

A named race can be selected before outdoor recording or linked to a genuine saved run afterward. Finishing and stopping early are separate outcomes. Recorded training bests use supported measured efforts; actual race/time-trial results are separate calibration evidence. Basic review does not require an invented race result.

AI Coach requires separate consent before messages and selected training context go to Anthropic. Responses can be wrong and usage limits apply. On-device Insights do not require AI sharing.

Movement Lab uses selected media for approximate two-dimensional fitness movement review, not medical assessment. Personal health records or real-person media are not needed for basic review.

Account deletion: More > Settings > Account & Legal > Request account deletion.
