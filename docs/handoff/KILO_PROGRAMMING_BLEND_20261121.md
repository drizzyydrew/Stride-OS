## What changes

- Person-first v2 strength-primary goals now have accumulation intent for the first three weeks of each six-week practice block and intensification intent for the last three. The existing independent3:1 recovery rhythm and present recovery limitations override that intent. Thus week4 can be recovery within an intended intensification period. Main exercises and history stay stable; a planned higher-repetition phase is not recorded as loss of strength.
- Practiced lifters can earn up to two weighted main-lift prescriptions at2×3–5, targetRPE8 and at least180seconds rest. Hybrid/secondary-strength support permits at most one, with race-priority, readiness, equipment and time limits. Beginners, foundation, recovery and short-time sessions retain manageable practice. Accessories, bodyweight, timed tasks and plyometrics keep their own purpose.
- First heavy entry needs the newest two distinct recent comparable preparatory performances, known setup/load units, completed working sets and actual manageable effort. Sorting occurs before qualification: a recent hard, skipped, incomplete, duplicate or unknown attempt cannot be hidden behind older good records. A new variation calibrates from its own history.
- The first successful heavy exposure can bridge into continued heavy practice without oscillating back to preparatory reps. A third set requires two successful heavy exposures in its own range and the time/recovery budget. Successfully completed, explicitly prescribed lighter recovery does not count as another heavy success.
- Future v2 workouts only seed a reference weight when the captured previous prescription matches today's rep range, target effort and setup. Entering heavier work or returning to higher reps clears an incompatible weight for calibration. Started workout payloads, completed prescriptions and actual sets remain intact; legacy v1 keeps its existing suggestion interface.
- When all matched working sets reach the upper rep target at the intended actual effort, guidance offers a small available load increase, roughly2–3%, conditional on execution and recovery. This is optional, not an automatic weight edit. If equipment jumps are too large, hold and consolidate the allowed reps. Below-minimum reps, clearly excessive effort or limited recovery prompt reduction/recalibration; missing effort earns no increase.
- Eleven explicitly identified strength presets use heavier priority main lifts while retaining supporting work. Card, filter duration, detail and launch use the same resolved prescription. Lifting experience, actual canonical plan phase, recovery and time gates apply to direct routes as well as recommendations, with a lighter alternative. Classic5×5 and beginner, runner-support, power, activation and recovery purposes retain their intended schemes. Already-started presets show and resume their captured prescription.

## What is deliberately not copied

KILO's5–6-set maximal-strength examples, final repetition-maximum/failure sets, forced weekly increases, specialist heavy-deadstop/wave/cluster methods, universal bodyweight strength ratios, fixed neural recruitment percentages and supplement protocols are not consumer defaults. Running speed still requires progressively tolerated running-specific work; lifting does not replace that stimulus.

Automatic step loading is not implemented in this revision. Current comparability assumes constant working loads; a genuine step-loading feature first needs a persisted strategy, ordered per-set targets and strategy-aware comparison. Simply accepting every mixed-load session could mistake failed or drop-set work for prescribed success. Existing editable actual sets remain usable without inventing a planned step sequence or transferring an estimated maximum.
